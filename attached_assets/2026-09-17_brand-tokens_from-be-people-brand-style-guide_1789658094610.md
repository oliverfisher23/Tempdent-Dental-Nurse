## Type

- Primary: **Ridley Grotesk** — modern sans-serif, nine weights with matching italics. Headlines Bold, sentence
  cased, auto tracking and leading. Body copy Regular.
- Accent: **Mono Spec** Regular, for captions and accents.
- Alternative, "for cases when you can't use the primary typeface": **Calibri** Bold and Regular.
- Licence: not stated in the guide. Ridley Grotesk is a commercial typeface — assume licensed to Marriott, not to
  us. Use Calibri, or a system stack, in anything we produce unless the client confirms otherwise.

## Logo rules

- The logo must always include the TM symbol on the bottom right side
- Never smaller than 18 px or 0.25 inches high
- Never modified in any way; use the height of the "e" as the clearspace reference
- Locking up with other logos: add a vertical line and clearspace; other logos should not compete with the Be logo,
  except brand and Bonvoy logos; exceptions need approval from peoplebrand@marriott.com

## Brand pillars and "Be Plus" extensions

Pillars: Begin. Belong. Become.
Approved extensions: Be curious. Be courageous. Be connected. Be inspired. Be empowered. Be an explorer. Be
inclusive. Be positive. Be purposeful. Be a leader. Be authentic. Be bold. Be limitless. Be you.

Formatting rules, as written:
- "Be" (even when translated) has a capital first letter, all others lower case
- "Be" must be followed by one lowercase word, except when referencing a Marriott brand
- "a" or "an" may be used between "Be" and the extension in rare instances
- Each extension ends with a full stop
- Be plus the extension creates a complete sentence
- They generally appear in groups of three, ending with "Be you."
- Anything beyond the approved list needs approval from peoplebrand@marriott.com

## Voice and tone, in the guide's own words

"our People Brand voice and tone should read as your older cousin doling out career advice, helping you find a job
and spruce up your resume. We are the insider, telling real-life stories in meaningful ways. We are informal,
conversational, truthful, and clear — think less jargon, more candid. We are respectful and positive, but
irreverent and honest. When in doubt, say it in less words and most importantly, say what you mean."

We are: warm, welcoming, kind, insider, real, authentic, informal, conversational, clear, plain-spoken, respectful,
positive, supportive, helpful, candid, inclusive.
We are not: corporate spin, jargon, stuffy, bureaucracy, disrespectful, boring, wishy-washy, exclusive, pretentious.

## Photography

- People: "candid, real people doing real jobs"
- Properties: "bright, transparent, natural light, not cluttered"
- Brand elements: "modern, fresh and informal... allow for clear space between photos and text — use negative space purposefully"
- Icons: only with a clear purpose, never as decoration
