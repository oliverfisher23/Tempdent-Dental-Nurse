---
name: Dietary review boundaries
description: Approval and safety boundaries for using the dietary learning-design recommendation in future work.
---

- Treat the dietary learning-design specification as a proposal, not authority to change the app, signed-off copy or filmed briefing.
  **Why:** the user explicitly commissioned review and specification only; proposed copy and sample learner answers have not been approved.
  **How to apply:** distinguish source-observed facts, recommendations and employer-confirmed content when using the document; implementation requires a separate explicit assignment.

- Keep ingredient matching separate from employer-approved preparation/service clearance.
  **Why:** the learning challenge should require evidence and reasoning without implying that a simplified recipe proves freedom from cross-contact. Inventing supplier declarations or a chef's assurance would remove the uncertainty by fabricating evidence.
  **How to apply:** unresolved preparation questions remain a hold, not a safe-to-serve result. The finite learning exercise needs approved evidence or an explicitly agreed narrower completion claim before implementation.
